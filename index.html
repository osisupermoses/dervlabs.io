Live / Events & Hospitality

StrictlyByIV

An all-in-one event planning platform and marketplace for Nigeria. Hosts plan their events and book the people who make them happen, from planners and vendors to venues. Providers get a storefront, bookings and secure payouts.

  • Plan events with budgets and checklists
  • Discover and book verified planners, vendors and venues
  • Manage guest lists for every event
  • Secure payments, with provider payouts released after the event
  • Coordinate teams and message collaborators in one place
  • Provider profiles, galleries and booking analytics

DervLabs Ltd is a technology consultancy and venture studio headquartered in Nigeria. We work where engineering rigour meets product vision, building software that solves real problems for real people.

We are both a parent company and a creative engine. We incubate independent ventures across verticals, and every product under the DervLabs umbrella is held to the same standard of craft, reliability and long-term thinking.

Whether we are shipping for a client or growing one of our own products, the bar stays the same: engineering that scales, design that communicates and software that lasts.

  • We ship our own products

    We build, launch and support our own ventures. The advice we give clients comes from running software in production ourselves.

  • Built for local reality

    Naira payments, patchy networks, budget phones and trust gaps are design constraints from day one, not afterthoughts.

  • Built to last

    We favour clear architecture, automated testing and maintainable code, so what we hand over keeps working long after launch.
